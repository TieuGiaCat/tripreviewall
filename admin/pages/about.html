About Tripreviewall

Tripreviewall is an independent review site for Hawaii tours. We're not a booking platform, and we don't get paid to feature any specific operator — our revenue comes from a small commission when you book through one of the links on our site, at no extra cost to you.

What makes us different

Most tour-booking sites are optimized to get you to book quickly, which means the reviews you see are often curated to look positive. We do the opposite: we aggregate ratings from every platform we can find for a given tour — TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide, FareHarbor and Google Maps — and show the full distribution, 1-star through 5-star, without hiding the parts that don't sell.

How we work

  • We pull scores from multiple platforms into one honest, aggregated number.
  • We tag reviews as "Verified Booking" only when we've confirmed the reviewer actually took the tour.
  • We don't delete negative reviews — we only remove content that violates our policy (spam, harassment, off-topic posts), never reviews we simply disagree with.
  • Every tour page carries an editorial "Our Verdict" — a short, plain-language assessment written by our team, not generated from the operator's own marketing copy.

Who's behind this

Tripreviewall is operated by Popotours. Our small editorial team researches and rates Hawaii tours across Oahu, Maui, Kauai and the Big Island, and updates listings as new review data becomes available.
