Affiliate Disclosure

Effective October 3, 2026

Tripreviewall earns money through affiliate links. When you click a booking link on this site and then book, we may receive a commission from the tour operator or booking platform. This never adds a fee or changes the price you pay.

Our affiliate partners

Tripreviewall, operated by Popotours, currently participates in affiliate or referral programs with:

  • FareHarbor — the booking system many Hawaii operators use on their own websites. "Check Availability" buttons take you to the operator's own FareHarbor booking page.
  • Viator
  • GetYourGuide
  • TripAdvisor

Commission rates differ between partners. That is exactly why we keep how we rate tours separate from how we are paid.

Which links are affiliate links

  • Booking buttons on tour pages — "Check Availability", the "Also available on" buttons and the booking options on mobile.
  • Links to FareHarbor, Viator, GetYourGuide or TripAdvisor inside our articles.

These links are marked as sponsored in the page code, and every booking box carries a short reminder that we may earn a commission. Links between pages of this site (for example "View Tour") are not affiliate links.

How this affects our reviews

It doesn't. Ratings, rating breakdowns and our "Our Verdict" notes are written independently of any commission:

  • We don't accept payment from operators or platforms to be listed, to rank higher, or to change a rating or verdict.
  • A tour that pays us a higher commission is never rated or ranked more favorably because of it.
  • We show low ratings and critical points alongside high ones — 5-star and 1-star alike.
  • Where a tour is sold on several platforms, we show each available option so you can choose where to book.

Where our ratings come from

The star ratings and review counts on tour pages are collected by our team from the public listings on Google Maps, TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide and Viator, and are checked periodically, so they may not reflect same-day changes on each platform. The overall rating is the average of those sources, weighted by how many reviews each has. Google Maps comments are summarized in our own words rather than quoted. We do not currently publish reviews submitted directly to Tripreviewall.
