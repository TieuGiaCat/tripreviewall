Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated September 15, 2026 — placeholder text, needs confirmation from counsel before launch.

Tripreviewall (operated by Popotours) participates in affiliate programs with FareHarbor, TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide and Viator. This means that when you click a booking link on our site and complete a purchase, we may earn a commission from the tour operator or platform, at no additional cost to you.

How this affects what you see

It doesn't. Our aggregated ratings, rating breakdowns, and editorial "Our Verdict" assessments are produced independently of any affiliate relationship. We do not accept payment to feature a tour more prominently, alter a rating, or remove a negative review. A tour paying a higher commission rate (for example, through FareHarbor) is never ranked or rated more favorably because of it.

Which links are affiliate links

  • Booking buttons on Tour Detail pages ("Check Availability," "Also available on…")
  • Tour cards embedded inside blog articles
  • The Transportation page's inquiry-based referral (if applicable)

Reviews and ratings

We aggregate ratings from TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide, FareHarbor and a manually-summarized Google Maps snapshot. First-party reviews collected directly on Tripreviewall are tagged "Verified Booking" only when we've confirmed the reviewer completed a booking; we do not remove negative reviews except for policy violations (spam, harassment, off-topic content).

Questions

Contact us at contact@tripreviewall.com with any questions about this disclosure.
