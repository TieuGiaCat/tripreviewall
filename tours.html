Hawaii's tour market spans four main islands, each with a different mix of operators and activity types. Oahu carries the largest share of listings we track (139 tours), driven by Honolulu-area snorkel, dive and Pearl Harbor operators. Big Island follows with a strong concentration of manta ray night snorkels and volcano tours, while Maui and Kauai skew toward boat charters and helicopter/air tours respectively. We update this list continuously as new tours are added and existing ones are re-checked.
