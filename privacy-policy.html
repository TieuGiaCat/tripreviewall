Privacy Policy

Last updated September 15, 2026 — placeholder framework, needs review by counsel before launch (this is not legal advice).

Information we collect

When you use the contact form, newsletter signup, or transportation inquiry form, we collect the information you provide (such as name, email, phone number, and message content) to respond to your inquiry. We do not require account creation, and we do not collect payment information — all bookings happen on the tour operator's or affiliate partner's own platform (FareHarbor, TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide, Viator).

Affiliate link tracking

We log aggregate click counts on affiliate booking links (which tour, which platform, which page) to understand which tours are most useful to readers. This tracking does not use cookies or identify individual visitors.

How we use your information

  • To respond to contact, newsletter, or transportation inquiries
  • To improve which tours and articles we prioritize covering
  • We do not sell personal information to third parties

Third-party links

Our site links to third-party booking platforms (FareHarbor, TripAdvisor, GetYourGuide, Viator) and Google Maps. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review before providing information to them.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@tripreviewall.com.
